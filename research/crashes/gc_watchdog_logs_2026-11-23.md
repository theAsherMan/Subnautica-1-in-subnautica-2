- **Where the time goes.** Every lobby→world LoadMap has one silent gap: from `LogAudio: Display: Audio Device unregistered from world 'None'.` to `LogRenderer: Forcing update for all mesh draw commands: SkyLight change`. This is the GC purge of the lobby (FinishDestroy wait).
  - It is a continuous distribution, and the crash is its tail: on loads that succeeded the max was 16.6 s (15 of 53 purges ≥ 10 s, 5 ≥ 15 s), then 20.05 and 20.06 s on the two crashes of that set. At 20 s the watchdog (`gc.MaxTimeForFinishDestroyGC:20`) aborts.
  - Consequence for tests: measure the purge seconds of every load (a continuous number), not only crash/no-crash. A few runs then show an effect that a ~4-8 % crash rate would hide.
- **Correlation with time since the lobby LoadMap started** (`LogLoad: LoadMap: …L_ClientLobby` → world `LoadMap`; 53-load set):
  - All 15 purges ≥ 10 s and both crashes happened 17–51 s after it.
  - Every load ≥ 52 s after it purged in ≤ 0.3 s: 22 loads, 52.5–259.9 s.
  - Both loads that came back to the lobby from a game (second lobby visit) purged in 0.006 s.
  - Below 50 s the result is mixed: 17–18 s loads purged anywhere from 0.05 to 16.6 s.
  - Short boots (game start → lobby ≤ 16 s, warm disk cache) purged in ≤ 0.06 s; every purge ≥ 10 s had boot → lobby ≥ 23.7 s.
  - **Boot → lobby time is the strongest single predictor found so far (LOGGED 2026-11-23, 55 loads).** Consecutive runs warm the disk cache, so boot time and purge both shrink: the three census runs, back-to-back, gave boot 45.6 / 25.4 / 13.1 s and purge 12.66 / 2.42 / 0.008 s. Long boots coincide with many `LogShaderLibrary ... blocking wait for shader preload` warnings, i.e. slow IO. Working idea (UNTESTED): the FinishDestroy wait is the render thread / IO still busy with boot-time loading (shader preload, streaming), so `DetachFence`s of the lobby-load garbage are not passed yet.
