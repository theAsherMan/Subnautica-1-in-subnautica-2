# GC-watchdog crash (lobby to world load)

Sources: 00_overview.md "State of play", 00_overview.md "Next steps" (split 2026-11-28; old text: git show 7221ba9:research/00_overview.md). The summary below only restates what the moved notes say; the original rows and items follow it.

## Summary

- **Symptom:** loading from the lobby into a world (`L_Main`, also `L_SN2World`) can end in a GC watchdog crash after about 20 s in the lobby purge ("Spent more than 20.00s on routing FinishDestroy"). Frequency LOGGED 2026-11-23: 4/49 logged loads on L_SN2World; the purge time is continuous (median 0.13 s, 15/53 at 10 s or more).
- **Status:** NOT fixed. Crashed again 2026-11-18 with all our Lua mods disabled, so our polling is not the cause. User decision 2026-11-18: the "wait 60 s at the menu" workaround is withdrawn; this is a bug to fix.
- **Best explanation so far (LOGGED 2026-11-23):** boot to lobby time predicts the purge: 16 s or less (warm cache) always purged in 0.06 s or less; every purge of 10 s or more and all 3 crashes had 23.7 s or more. Not tested as a cause.
- **Verified facts:** the slow purge is game-thread work (GameThread 99.7 % busy, other threads idle; VERIFIED for one sampled run 2026-11-24). The lobby has only 32 live SphereComponents (LOGGED 2026-11-23), so the watchdog objects are invisible to `FindAllOf`.

## Dead ends

- `LogGarbage Verbose` and `-trace` do not work in Shipping builds: **FAILED (2026-11-24)**.
- The ~22.8k queue is not a SphereComponent set (only 148 exist, all unflagged at travel time): LOGGED 2026-11-24.
- A 5 ON + 5 OFF A/B in sequence is confounded by cache warming; the partial A/B (8 runs) of 2026-11-24 was too small to separate the arms: LOGGED 2026-11-24.

## Candidate fixes not applied

- None are recorded as applied. The sampled-crash and render-pipe series (H6 to H8) is in the 2026-11-25 file listed below.
