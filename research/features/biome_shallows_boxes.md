## Remaining differences and calibration direction (UNTESTED as changes) — OPEN (2026-11-27)
1. Brightness is 30 % of SN1 (lum 42-44 vs 136-144) at 3 and 12 m: the water absorbs too much light. Raise BiomeExtinction toward 1.0, especially the skylight extinction (0.6,0.761,0.8) toward about (0.8,0.9,0.95), and/or reduce the sky height-fog density (0.190 -> 0.098 gave a clearer look earlier).
2. Hue too violet/navy, no green: SN1 aquamarine (G 157 vs B 191 at natures_table; SN2 G 43 vs B 71). Raise the green share of BiomeAlbedo from (0.1,0.295,1.0) toward about (0.15,0.55,1.0) (SN1 cyan G/B about 0.8, SN2 0.6).
3. Sight distance too short (haze swallows 30-60 m): raise WaterFogDistance well above 7500 or lower FogThickness (10) toward SN1's about 60 m flat-blue fade; keep the extinction change small so deep views stay blue.
4. Depth: at 51 m (ravine) the sunlit orange rock is not attenuated to blue; terrain albedo is flat rust-orange (no SN1 texture), which also pulls B/R down everywhere. Calibrate the ravine last; the biggest lever there is the terrain material colour.
5. The 42 SN1 boxes: make them active (runtime-set `BiomeRegionVolumeType`=1 as for the ocean) to test a local per-biome look.
Change one parameter group per run and compare B/R (target 1.5-2.3), luminance (target about 140) and colour distance (target < 60) with `src/tools/samples.py analyze`.

## Mechanism of the water look (VERIFIED 2026-11-27, UE4SS.log `UWPROBE uw REGION ... MAT` lines + cooked L_Main export; condensed)
- A water region's look is its `WaterStaticMeshComponent` (`/Engine/EngineMeshes/s_cube_1cm`, huge cube) drawn with a MID of `MI_OceanVolume`. In L_Main that MID, `MI_OceanVolume_Biome_BPDefined_0`, is a SERIALIZED sub-object of the cooked map (export in `<ModkitDir>/scratch_bp/lmain_json/L_Main.json`, class MaterialInstanceDynamic, outer = the region component): 13 scalars (`BiomeExtinctionDistanceDirectionalLight` 12.5, `...Skylight` 17.5, `WaterFogDistance` 7500, `WaterFogThickness` 10, `BaseWaterFogThickness` 4, `WaterFogOpacityWeight` 1, ...), 6 vectors (`BiomeExtinctionColorDirectionalLight` = `...Skylight` = (0.907,0.945,0.955), `BiomeAlbedo` (0.1,0.295,1), ...) and `BasePropertyOverrides.BlendMode = BLEND_TranslucentGreyTransmittance`.
- In L_SN2World every region (the ocean's CAT_0, the standalone `BP_UWEOceanBiomeRegionActor_C_1`, all 42 boxes) has only a generic runtime `MID_MI_OceanVolume_<n>` with 2 scalars (`CurrentWaterBodyIndex`, `CurrentWaterBodyBlendType`, written by the tracker); the cooked `L_SN2World` has no such MID export.
- The MID is made by native `AUWEWaterBiomeRegionActor::RecreateMaterials`, called only from the Blueprint `UserConstructionScript` (the construction script runs in the editor and the MID is saved into the level; a cooked placed actor does not re-run it; the modkit C++ is a stub, so nothing is baked). Native behaviour is inferred (no source).
- Camera classification fine: `WaterSubsystem.GetCameraUnderwaterDepth` 179.6 (L_SN2World, cam z -175) vs 156.2 (L_Main), `IsUnderwaterPostProcessEnabled` true in both; cooked `WaterBodyOceanComponent` + collision identical. Secondary difference (effect not isolated): `BP_Ocean_C` runtime `BodySize` (0,0,0) vs (1e6,1e6,1e5), `WaterLinePPMThreshold` 0 vs 200, `UnderwaterStateUpdateRate` 0 vs 0.1, `bDirty` true vs false.
- Equal in both maps: TimeOfDay 0.2542 (6:06, `UWETimeOfDayComponent` InitialHour 7, 24-min day), sun 7.5 / 6500 K, skylight 1.25 real-time capture, sun light-function MID (`MI_LF_CausticsLightFunction_v2`), sky post-process exposure (bias 0.5, min -10, max 0, bloom 0.75), cvars. Only the unused Moon's `bEnableLightFunctionOnLumen` differs.
- Screenshots: L_Main `20261127_170201_uwprobe_lmain/shots/00_pod_underwater_side_k0.png`, `01_pod_10m_below_k0.png` uniformly saturated blue, bright blue sky with white clouds; L_SN2World `20261127_170840_uwprobe_sn2/shots/00_pod_underwater_side_k0.png` brownish-grey fog with a dark navy sky; the older `20261119_212503_sn2world/shots/02_pod_underwater_side_k0.png` (no terrain, no SN1 regions) is already dark navy, so the defect predates the SN1 content.
