### Design chosen for the first test (UNTESTED; mod `SN2EmptyWorld`, chunk 2)
1. **Cells never load:** the ModActor spawned in the **lobby** runs `ExecuteConsoleCommand("wp.Runtime.UpdateStreamingSources 0")` and `("wp.Runtime.HLOD 0")` and stores the read-back
   (`GetConsoleVariableIntValue`) in public vars. Expected (UE source, UNTESTED here): L_Main's WP subsystem starts with an empty source list and never refills it → no spatial cell loads
   anywhere; only always-loaded content (persistent level + always-loaded data-layer cells). Open risks: SN2 may block console commands from BP; the game mode may wait for the spawn
   proxy's streaming to complete (loading screen hang).
2. **Always-loaded content purged on the first frame:** the ModActor in L_Main destroys every actor whose name contains `_UAID_` unless its class is on the keep-list
   (lifepod set, ocean set, sky, invisible managers above, any `PlayerStart` subclass), plus `UWEWorldPopGlobalResourcesDataStorage`, plus any `UWEAIPawn`/`UWEAICharacter`
   (safety net, counted). Repeats every 1 s; `CollectGarbage` when something was destroyed.
3. Measured by a read-only Lua census (`SN2Census`): cvar values, loaded `_Generated_` levels, actor counts by class, remaining `_UAID_` actors.
- Not avoidable with this design: `L_Main.umap` itself (persistent 1,668 actors) still loads before being purged. Only replacing `L_Main` with our own map avoids that.
