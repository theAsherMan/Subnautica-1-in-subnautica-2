## Triangle-shaped holes: SOLVED
- **VERIFIED (2026-11-23, user F1 bug reports `<Workspace>/bugshots/20261123_181123_001`, `..._181156_002`, `..._181232_003`, user: "there is a hole into the world", "there are a lot of them").** Single-triangle holes in rock crevices about 30-45 m W/SW of the pod (`SM_origin_tile_1_1`, `movementMode` 4). 001: camera UE (-1636,-2279,-2161), looking up, sky and clouds visible through a clean triangle, crosshair hit 2.09 m at UE (-1769.8,-2340.1,-2011.4). 002: camera UE (-2813,-3308,-2448), looking down, dark triangular gap, trace `hit=false` (collision missing too); vertical rock faces also showed streaks of stretched planar-UV texture. 003: camera UE (-2092,-4313,-2597), looking up at a ceiling with one thin triangle hole, hit 3.72 m on `SM_origin_tile_1_1`.
- **Cause (2026-11-23, offline analysis of the origin OBJs): non-manifold edges from the surface-nets mesher, dropped by the DynamicMesh at import.** 1,445 edges shared by 4 triangles (checkerboard cell faces); GeometryScript `append_buffers_to_mesh` keeps only 2 per edge. The 535 "interior open edges" counted earlier were a separate rounding artifact (`research/sn1/README.md`). Not a tile seam (tiles are 125 m).
- **Fix (extractor `make_manifold`, vertex splitting):** re-extracted (`origin_fix1`, 35 s): bad edges per pass [1450, 4, 0], +1,527 vertices, `interior_open_plus_nonmanifold` 0, 1,908,950 triangles. Imported replacing the 16 `SM_origin_tile_*` actors in place: log `triangles in OBJ files vs kept by DynamicMesh: lost 0 (must be 0)`, `RELOAD CHECK PASSED`. Cooked with `cook_install.sh SN2EmptyWorld 2` (5 min 12 s, 17 packages), `.ucas` 348,352,096 B.
- **VERIFIED (2026-11-23, autotest `holes_recheck`, bugshot engine shots `<Workspace>/bugshots/20261123_190147_001`, `..._190155_001`, `..._190203_001`).** At the three report camera poses (within about 1.5 deg) the crosshair ray hits `SM_origin_tile_1_1` in all three (`hit:true`, 2.7 m, 1.5 m, 4.9 m); continuous rock, no sky triangle. Jagged cube edges and texture streaks remain (streaks: planar XY UVs, `research/sn1/README.md`). The 8-direction plus up/down shots at 003 (`shots/03_r003_8_k0..k9.png`) were taken but not examined in detail.
- Dead end: **FAILED (2026-11-23)** hypothesis "tile seam / cells dropped at internal chunk boundaries": the holes are far from tile borders; the offline analysis places the cause in non-manifold edges.
